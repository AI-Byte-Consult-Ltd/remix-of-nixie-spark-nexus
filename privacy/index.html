    Privacy Policy

    How AI Byte Consult Ltd. — an applied-AI company, not a single Meta integration — collects, uses, and protects personal data across our website, Telegram bot, NICS Trading Ecosystem, and connected Meta/Threads account.

    Last updated: November 2026Company: AI Byte Consult Ltd.Reg. No.: 201718190

    Who We Are

    AI Byte Consult Ltd. ("we", "us", "our", the "Company") is a Bulgaria-registered applied-AI company. We are not a single-purpose app — our products include this website (aibyteconsult.com), NICS Trading Ecosystem (Telegram bot, Mini App and trading-signal service), NICS Real Estate (estate.aibyteconsult.com), NICS Multimedia, and our official Meta/Threads presence, which we use to automatically publish our own trading performance and journal updates.

    This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect across these products, why, who we share it with, and the rights you have over it. It applies wherever you interact with AI Byte Consult Ltd. — not only through Meta or Threads.

    What Data We Collect

    Website visitors: standard technical data generated by any web request (IP address, browser type, pages visited) as processed by our hosting and CDN infrastructure. If you use the contact form, we collect the name, email address, and message you submit.

    Newsletter subscribers: the email address you provide when subscribing.

    Telegram bot & NICS Trading Ecosystem Mini App users: your Telegram user ID, username and chat ID, subscription/access status, trading-signal interactions (accept/decline, delivery status), demo-journal entries you submit, and rewards/check-in activity where that feature is used.

    Personal cabinet users: the Telegram profile fields provided by the Telegram Login Widget (see "Personal Cabinet & Telegram Login" below), and, only if you complete the academy and choose to receive a certificate, the full name you explicitly provide for it.

    Contact via phone, Viber or WhatsApp: whatever information you choose to share with us in that conversation.

    Meta / Threads Platform Data

    AI Byte Consult Ltd. operates its own Threads account to automatically publish real, already-closed NICS Trading Ecosystem performance statistics and journal entries. To do this, our backend holds a Meta access token and basic account identifiers for that one, company-owned account — this is "Platform Data" under Meta's Platform Terms.

    This is an outbound publishing integration, not a "Login with Meta/Threads" feature for site visitors: we do not ask website or Telegram users to connect their personal Meta or Threads account to use our Services, and we do not read or collect data from any Meta account other than our own.

    The access token and related Platform Data are stored on our own infrastructure (see "Data Sharing" below) and are never sold, and never shared with any party except the infrastructure providers strictly necessary to operate the integration.

    Personal Cabinet & Telegram Login

    The personal cabinet at aibyteconsult.com/cabinet lets you sign in with the Telegram Login Widget, using the same Telegram account you use with the NICS Trading Ecosystem bot. Telegram provides us your Telegram user ID, first and last name, username, and profile photo URL as part of that sign-in — we do not receive your phone number or any other Telegram account data through this flow.

    Signing in issues a session token stored in your browser's local storage (not a cookie), valid for up to 7 days and automatically renewed while you stay signed in. Signing out, clearing your browser storage, or letting the session expire ends it; we do not keep a separate server-side session record beyond what the token itself encodes.

    If you complete every lesson of the NICS Trading Ecosystem Academy, we ask you — via a message from the Telegram bot — for the full name you want printed on your certificate. Once issued, the certificate is stored with that name and is viewable by anyone who has its direct verification link. This is intentional: like a diploma or professional certification, it is only meaningful if it can be independently verified. We only ever store the name you explicitly provide for this purpose — never your Telegram profile name.

    The cabinet also displays any referral cashback balance you have earned through our existing 30% referral-commission program; this does not involve collecting any new category of financial data beyond what the Telegram bot already tracks for that program.

    How We Use Your Data

    To operate the Services you use — deliver trading signals, respond to inquiries, process newsletter subscriptions, run the rewards/check-in feature, and publish our own automated performance updates to Threads and other channels.

    To communicate with you where you have opted in — for example, Telegram bot notifications you have not blocked, or newsletter updates you have not unsubscribed from.

    To maintain security, prevent abuse, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

    We do not use your personal data to train third-party AI models, and we do not sell it.

    Data Sharing & Processors

    We share data only with service providers strictly necessary to run the Services, under confidentiality obligations, never for their own independent marketing use:

    • Telegram (Bot API) — to deliver bot messages, based on the chat you initiate.

    • Meta (Threads API) — to publish our own account's automated posts, as described above.

    • Oracle Cloud Infrastructure — hosts the servers and database that store the data described in this policy.

    • Cloudflare — stores encrypted, access-controlled backups of that database.

    • Supabase — powers the website's contact form and public market-data widgets, on a separate project from the trading backend.

    • Vantage (our MT5 broker data feed) — supplies market price data only; it does not receive personal data about you.

    We do not sell, rent, or otherwise trade your personal data to third parties for their own marketing purposes.

    Data Retention

    We keep personal data for as long as your account or subscription is active, plus a reasonable period afterward to handle support requests, resolve disputes, and meet legal or accounting obligations. Automated database backups are rotated on a schedule (nightly backups for 14 days, weekly backups for 56 days) and are superseded by newer backups over time.

    Where you request deletion (see "Your Rights" below), we remove the personal data we hold about you from our active systems, subject to any data already contained in a backup snapshot taken before your request, which ages out on the schedule above.

    Security

    We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the data we hold, including restricted access to production systems, encrypted storage for sensitive credentials, and access-controlled backups. No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security of information transmitted to us.

    If we become aware of a security incident affecting your personal data, we will take reasonable steps to investigate and, where required by law, notify affected individuals and relevant authorities.

    Your Rights

    Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal data, to withdraw consent to marketing communications at any time, and to object to certain processing.

    You can unsubscribe from the newsletter with the link in any email, stop or leave the Telegram bot at any time, and revoke our Threads app's access to our connected account (not relevant to your own data, since we do not access visitor Meta accounts).

    Cookies & Tracking

    This website does not use cookies for analytics or advertising, and no third-party tracking or advertising scripts (such as Google Analytics or the Meta Pixel) run on it. Any cookie or local-storage use is limited to functional purposes, such as remembering a language preference or a signed-in session in the NICS Trading Ecosystem Mini App.

    Cookie-free website statistics: to understand which pages are useful and which channels bring visitors, we run our own privacy-friendly counter on our own server. For each page view and for a few actions (for example opening our Telegram bot, starting a trial, downloading the Windows app, sending a form or starting a checkout) it records the page address without any query parameters, the name of the referring website or campaign tag, the site language, device type (mobile, tablet or desktop) and the time. It does not set cookies or store anything in your browser, does not record what you type into forms, and does not store your IP address or browser details. To count unique visitors, a one-way code is computed from your IP address and browser together with a random value that is replaced every day, so a visitor cannot be recognised on another day or identified. The data is used only as totals, kept for up to 400 days, never sold or shared, and processed on the basis of our legitimate interest in running and improving the website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). You can switch it off on your device by opening any page of the site with ?notrack=1 at the end of the address.

    First-party usage statistics in the NICS Trading Ecosystem app (Telegram Mini App, web cabinet and Windows app): to improve the app we record which screens are opened and which features are used (for example "signal ordered" or "theme changed"), together with the platform, app version, language and theme. These records are stored on our own servers, linked to your NICS account only internally, never sold or shared, and reviewed only as totals (active users, popular screens). We do not record message texts, trade amounts, license keys, passwords or e-mail content. You can ask us to delete them together with the rest of your data.

    Children's Privacy

    Our Services, in particular trading-related features, are intended for users who are at least 18 years old or the age of legal majority in their jurisdiction. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will remove it.

    International Data Transfers

    AI Byte Consult Ltd. is registered in Bulgaria (EU) and our infrastructure providers operate globally. Where personal data is transferred outside the EU/EEA, we rely on the data-protection safeguards those providers make available (such as standard contractual clauses) to the extent applicable.

    Changes to This Policy

    We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes take effect immediately upon posting to this page. We encourage you to review it periodically.
