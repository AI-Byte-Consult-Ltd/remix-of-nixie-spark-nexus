Search for "AI trading software for Windows" and you get everything from free robots to expensive "AI systems" with screenshots of perfect equity curves. Before installing anything, it helps to know what kind of software you are looking at and what to check.

## Three kinds of software

| Type | What it does | Main risk |
|---|---|---|
| **Expert Advisor (EA) for MT4/MT5** | Runs inside the trading terminal and places trades automatically | It trades your money without asking; backtests are often over-fitted |
| **Signal and analysis app** | Analyses markets and shows setups; you decide and place the trade | You still need discipline to follow your risk rules |
| **Web platform in a desktop window** | The same service as the website, packaged as an app | Depends on the provider's servers and account security |

None of these is automatically better. The question is which one fits how much control you want to keep. We compare the first two in more detail in [trading signals vs automated trading](/blog/trading-signals-vs-automated-trading/).

## Eight things to check before installing

1. **Does it trade for you or advise you?** Know this before you connect anything to a broker account.
2. **Is there a public, complete track record?** All closed trades, losses included, ideally in R — not a curated screenshot. See [how to read the risk-to-reward numbers](/blog/risk-to-reward-ratio/).
3. **Where does the price data come from?** A named broker or data feed is a good sign; "proprietary AI data" with no source is not.
4. **Does it handle risk?** Look for stop losses on every idea and position sizing based on your balance, not a fixed lot.
5. **What does it need from you?** Analysis software should never ask for your broker password. An EA runs in your own terminal and also does not need it.
6. **Is the installer trustworthy?** Download only from the provider's own site or an official store. If Windows SmartScreen warns about an unsigned installer, an honest provider explains why.
7. **How does the license work?** How many computers, whether it works offline, what happens when the subscription ends.
8. **Is the price clear?** A trial price, a monthly price and how to cancel should be visible before payment.

## Red flags

- Guaranteed or "risk-free" profit
- A track record with almost no losing trades
- Pressure to deposit with a specific broker before you can see anything
- No company name, address or registration behind the product
