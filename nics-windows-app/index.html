    NICS Trading Ecosystem for Windows

    Your trading workspace on the desktop: live technical analysis, on-demand analysis of any supported instrument, the economic calendar and the verified track record in one window, without Telegram.

    Installer .exe, about 1.3 MB

    No Windows? Open the web dashboard

    What the app does

    Live technical analysis

    Every new setup with Entry zone, Stop Loss and TP1–TP4, the direction basis and risk notes, as soon as the engine publishes it.

    Order an analysis

    Ask for a fresh analysis of one instrument or an extra scan of all markets at any time.

    Trade lifecycle

    Follow each trade through TP, SL and breakeven updates and see the result in your history.

    Economic calendar

    High-impact events for the week, so you know when news can move your positions.

    Verified performance

    The same public track record as on the site: every closed strong setup, no cherry-picking.

    Academy and articles

    Lessons, the trading journal and articles inside the app, in five languages, light or dark theme.

    System requirements

    • Windows 10 or Windows 11, 64-bit
    • Microsoft Edge WebView2 (already part of Windows 11 and most Windows 10 PCs; the installer adds it if missing)
    • Internet connection
    • A NICS account: sign in with Telegram or by e-mail code

    How to install

    1. 1Click “Download for Windows” and open NICS-Trading-Ecosystem-Setup.exe.
    2. 2The installer is not code-signed yet, so Windows may show “Windows protected your PC”. Click “More info”, then “Run anyway”.
    3. 3Finish the setup and start NICS Trading Ecosystem from the Start menu or the desktop shortcut.
    4. 4Sign in with Telegram or with an e-mail code. With an active subscription your Pro key is filled in automatically.

    Pro license

    Pro comes with any active paid NICS Trading Ecosystem subscription, including the €1.99 7-day trial. There is no separate price for the app.

    Your personal key (NICS-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX) is in your account and is filled in automatically after sign-in.

    One key works on two computers. The app checks it once a day and keeps working offline for up to 7 days.

    The app is the property of AI Byte Consult Ltd. It is not sold or rented: the key gives a personal, non-transferable right to use it while your subscription or key is active.

    Questions

    Is the app free?

    Downloading and signing in is free. The trading screens need Pro, which comes with any active paid NICS Trading Ecosystem subscription.

    Does the app place trades for me?

    No. It delivers technical analysis; you decide whether to open a position with your broker.

    Why does Windows show a warning?

    The installer is not code-signed yet. The Microsoft Store version, signed by Microsoft, is being prepared.

    Do I need to update the app?

    The workspace inside the app loads the current version every time it starts, so new features arrive without reinstalling.

    Is there a Mac or mobile version?

    Not as a separate app yet. On Mac, phone or tablet use the web dashboard or the Telegram Mini App with the same account.

    Can I use one subscription on several devices?

    Yes: the web dashboard and Telegram everywhere, and the Windows app on up to two computers per key.
