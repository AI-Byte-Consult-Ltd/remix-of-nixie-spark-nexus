    Inspired by an experiment

    A fly that learns to trade, and what we took from it

    In September 2026, Coinbase engineer Alex Wormuth showed an unusual experiment. He took a digital model of a fruit fly brain (166,700 neurons and 25.6 million connections) and showed it a Bitcoin chart. The price was turned into an image for the fly's "eyes", and its reactions were translated into buy, sell or wait. A profit gave the fly "dopamine", a loss switched on aversion neurons, a "punishment". It traded with a virtual $100. The author is honest that he has not yet proven the fly learned to trade profitably. It is research, not a ready-made strategy.

    What caught our attention was the idea itself: a system that learns not only from the rules written for it, but also from the consequences of its own decisions. We brought this principle into NICS Trading Ecosystem.

    Source: bits.media

    The reward loop

    1. Setup
    2. Trade closes
    3. Result in R
    4. Dopamine or penalty
    5. New confidence

    How it works in our system

    The main analysis stays the same: technical analysis of gold, forex and crypto, an entry, a stop and four targets. Alongside it, a separate parallel stream runs every night: the reward engine. It takes all closed trades, groups them by setup type and calculates the real result in R. Setup types that ended in profit get "dopamine", and confidence in them rises slightly. Types that ended in a loss get a "penalty", and confidence goes down. The steps are small and capped, so one bad week cannot turn the system upside down. This stream has been running since 17 September 2026.

    Honest about results

    It is too early to say this has made the setups more profitable. All real results, good and bad, are in the Verified Track Record above. If the approach does not pay off, we will say so here.

    What's next

    We keep developing this stream. The digital fly is already in our GitHub repository, and we are connecting it now. It will first run in a separate sandbox, with no client money, and we will show how it does.

    Frequently asked questions

    What is NICS Trading Ecosystem?

    NICS Trading Ecosystem is an autonomous AI trading platform built by AI Byte Consult Ltd: a Windows app, a web dashboard, a Telegram bot with Mini App and a trading academy. It publishes structured trade setups with Entry, Stop Loss and four Take Profit targets, and keeps a public verified track record.

    Which markets does it cover?

    Gold and silver, nine forex pairs, Brent oil, Bitcoin and Ethereum, NVIDIA and Tesla, and the US100, US500 and GER40 indices. The engine scans them around the clock.

    Where does the price data come from?

    Prices come from Vantage's live MT5 feed. NICS Trading Ecosystem is an independent analysis service by AI Byte Consult Ltd and is not part of Vantage; the levels in an analysis match the prices you see at this broker.

    Do I have to accept every setup?

    No. Each setup arrives in the Windows app, the web dashboard or Telegram, and you accept or skip it. Nothing is executed automatically.

    Is the risk sizing personal to me?

    Yes. Position size, total exposure and correlated risk are calculated from your own account balance, currency and risk settings, set once in the Mini App and adjustable at any time.

    Does NICS guarantee profits?

    No. Trading involves substantial risk. NICS provides structured analytical scenarios and risk-management tools, not guaranteed returns, investment advice or automatic profit.
